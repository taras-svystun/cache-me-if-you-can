**Sonnet:** On day 45 you fall in the 31-to-60-day window, so a 15% restocking fee applies (as long as the frame is undamaged). That's 480 EUR, leaving you a refund of 2,720 EUR. Second, the police report must be filed within 48 hours, and yours was filed 3 days later. The other conditions (theft within 2 years of purchase, a police report) may apply, but the lock and timing requirements are not met. You can contact the service team at service@nimbus.example (9:00 to 17:00 CET, working days) if you want to discuss your case further.

**Sonnet:** Based on the policy, it looks like you wouldn't be covered under AB12. Going through each condition:

1. **Stolen within 2 years of purchase:** Unknown from your message. If it's within 2 years, this one is met.
2. **Locked with a Nimbus-approved D-lock:** Not met. A cable lock doesn't qualify.
3. **Police report within 48 hours:** Not met. Three days is outside the 48-hour window.

Since AB12 requires all conditions, two failures mean no cover. If you'd like to double-check your situation, you can email service@nimbus.example (working days, 9:00 to 17:00 CET).
