# Nimbus Bikes customer policy (made up for testing)

Nimbus Bikes is a fictional e-bike shop. Nothing here describes a real company.

## 1. Returns
1.1 You may return a Nimbus e-bike within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, if the bike has
been ridden less than 50 km and the frame is undamaged.
1.2 Between day 31 and day 60 we accept returns with a 15% restocking fee.
1.3 Accessories (lights, locks, mounts) can be returned within 14 days if unused and in the
original box.
1.4 Return shipping is free inside metropolitan France. Elsewhere in the EU it costs 49 EUR.
1.5 Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days of the bike reaching
our warehouse in Annecy.

## 2. Warranty
2.1 Frames carry a 7-year warranty against cracks and weld failure.
2.2 Motors and batteries carry a 2-year warranty, or 800 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
2.3 The warranty does not cover wear parts: tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes,
grips and saddles. A flat tyre is therefore not covered.
2.4 Damage from crashes, water immersion, or non-Nimbus chargers is not covered.
2.5 To claim, send the serial number, proof of purchase and a photo to the service team.

## 3. Shipping
3.1 Orders ship from Annecy within 2 working days.
3.2 Delivery inside metropolitan France takes 3 to 5 working days (Lyon: usually 3).
3.3 Delivery to other EU countries takes 5 to 9 working days.
3.4 We do not ship outside the EU.
3.5 Bikes arrive 90% assembled; the handlebar and pedals need fitting (tools included).

## 4. Orders and cancellation
4.1 You can cancel free of charge until the order ships.
4.2 After the order ships you cannot cancel it, but you can refuse delivery or use the 30-day
return in section 1.
4.3 Custom-colour frames cannot be cancelled once production has started (day 3 after order).

## 5. Products
5.1 Nimbus City: 504 Wh removable battery, hub motor, 25 km/h assist limit, range 60-110 km.
5.2 Nimbus Trail: 720 Wh integrated battery, mid-drive motor, 25 km/h assist limit (EU law),
range 50-130 km depending on terrain.
5.3 Nimbus Cargo: 2 x 504 Wh batteries, carries up to 180 kg including rider.
5.4 The serial number is engraved under the bottom bracket, the part of the frame where the
pedal cranks meet.
5.5 We sell phone mounts and Apple Watch mounts; we do not sell child seats.

## 6. Service
6.1 Service your e-bike every 6 months or every 1,500 km, whichever comes first.
6.2 The first service within 3 months of purchase is free at any Nimbus partner shop.
6.3 To reset the display, hold the power and minus buttons together for 5 seconds.
6.4 Firmware updates are installed at service visits; we do not offer over-the-air updates.

## 7. Discounts
7.1 Students get 10% off bikes (not accessories) with a valid student card.
7.2 Discounts cannot be combined with sale prices or with each other.
7.3 Employer cycle-to-work schemes are accepted; ask for a quote by email.

## 8. Theft and insurance
8.1 Policy AB12 is our theft cover: if your bike is stolen within 2 years of purchase, we replace
it for 25% of its current price, if it was locked with a Nimbus-approved D-lock and you file a
police report within 48 hours.
8.2 Policy AB13 is our accident cover for frame damage from crashes, 9 EUR per month.
8.3 Policy numbers are case-sensitive. "Ab12" is not a valid Nimbus policy.
8.4 Neither policy covers batteries removed and left outside a locked space.

## 9. Privacy
9.1 We keep order data for 5 years for tax reasons, then delete it.
9.2 We never sell customer data. Ride data from the display stays on the bike.

## 10. Contact
10.1 Service team: service@nimbus.example, working days 9:00 to 17:00 CET.
10.2 Answers here describe Nimbus policy only. For legal advice, ask a lawyer.
